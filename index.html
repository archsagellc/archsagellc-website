Hardware

Production Infrastucture

Archsage LLC operates on purpose-built hardware, enterprise networking equipment, and a custom liquid cooling system designed for stability, security, long-duration rendering workloads, and remote capabilities for monitoring, loading, and delivering scenes.

Enterprise Rendering Platform

Built around the ASUS Pro WS WRX80E-SAGE SE WIFI and AMD Threadripper Pro 5995WX, Archsage's rendering workstation is designed for reliability, sustained performance, and long-duration workloads. We consistently hit 80k vsamples per minute on V-Ray Benchmark, you can see our scores on the public board The platform includes 256 GB of ECC Registered Memory, an NVIDIA RTX 3080 Ti graphics processor, and dedicated high-speed NVMe storage to support demanding rendering projects.

Custom Liquid Cooling Loop

A fully customized liquid cooling loop provides stable operating temperatures during extended rendering sessions. The system utilizes Optimus CPU and GPU water blocks, an EKWB X560M radiator, Aquacomputer pump/reservoir, sensors, Aquasuite monitoring software, and nine Noctua industrial-grade cooling fans to deliver efficient thermal management under continuous load. Our custom cooling loop is oversized by design for long duration runs, we can run Prime95 indefinetly with the 5995wx comfortably sitting at 50C.

Enterprise Grade Network

Archsage's network is designed to support secure project transfers, remote administration, and dependable connectivity. The environment includes 1 Gigabit service feed, Ubiquiti enterprise networking equipment, and WireGuard remote access tunnel setup with Unifi UI. Utilizing all Ubiquiti equipment allows Archsage to ensure that wireless infrastructure stays separate from workstation operations and allows us to monitor network traffic thanks to Unifi UI. Your assets are safe with Archsage.
